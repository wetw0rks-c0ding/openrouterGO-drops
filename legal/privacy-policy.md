# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-02

This app is unofficial and is not affiliated with OpenRouter.

## What the app stores

- Your OpenRouter management key, in the operating system keychain.
- An encrypted local database holding alerts, budgets, preferences, and BYOK references.

## Network requests

Requests go directly to `openrouter.ai`. The app contains no analytics, no
advertisements, and no crash reporting.

## Developer access

The developer receives no account data.

## Deleting your data

Use "Sign out and delete local data" in the app, or uninstall the app.

## Unofficial status

This app is unofficial and is not affiliated with OpenRouter.
